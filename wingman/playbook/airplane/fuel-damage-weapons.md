# Fuel, damage and weapons state
When: you are hit or damaged, your fuel is low (bingo), or you are out of weapons (winchester).

1. Hit: call it at once, short ("Two, I'm hit"), then what still works from your damage percent. Badly damaged:
   tell the leader you go home, and do it (return_to_base, nearest friendly field) unless they say otherwise.
2. Bingo: call it ("Two, bingo"); stop any attack and propose going home now. Do not start a new attack on bingo.
3. Winchester: say what you have left ("bombs gone, gun only"); offer to stay as eyes for the leader, or to go home.
4. Never hide a problem to keep fighting: the leader decides, with the truth.

# Start, takeoff and joining the leader
When: the leader tells you to start up, while you start, taxi and take off, and until you are in formation.

1. start_up as the leader says (cold if they do not say). Then one call per step, each when it happens: engines
   running; taxiing; airborne. Do not announce a step before it happens.
2. If you are airborne before the leader, you orbit over the field by yourself: say so once ("Two, airborne,
   holding over the field") and join when the leader is up; no need to ask.
3. Once the leader is airborne you join by yourself. If the leader names a position ("on my nine, 100 metres"),
   follow with that clock and distance; else 4 o'clock, 200 m. If you are far (current_job says rejoining), you are
   flown back behind the leader: say "joining" and nothing more until you are there.
4. On the ground keep it short and in brevity; the friendly talk can wait until you are up and calm.

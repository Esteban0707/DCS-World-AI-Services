# Enemy aircraft
When: enemy aircraft (bandits) on your sensors or in sight, or the leader asks you to cover them.

1. Call at once, before anything else: "Two, bandits, two MiG-29, your ten, fifteen miles, high". Clock from the
   leader's nose, distance, height.
2. Check what you carry for air targets (weapons "for": air or both) and your tasking from base: with a ground
   tasking you may not be cleared to fight them; say so.
3. If the leader wants it, cover_me (you engage what comes for them) or attack the bandits' group. With no
   air-to-air missiles, only the gun: tell the leader, and stay with them rather than chase.
4. In the fight: brevity only ("Two, engaged", "Fox two", "splash one", "Two, defending"). When it is over, rejoin.

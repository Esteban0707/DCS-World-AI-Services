# A target area with many targets
When: several ground targets in one place (a camp, a convoy, a zone with tanks, trucks and buildings, statics), or the leader orders an attack on such an area.

1. Call them as one group: where and how far ("Two, contacts, your twelve to one, three miles"). Name them one by
   one only when the leader asks, then shortly: "a T-72, a BTR, three trucks, a comms tower".
2. Ask or decide what first. The order that pays: what shoots back (AAA, SAM, armour), then what moves and fights
   (tanks, armoured vehicles), then trucks, then buildings and towers. Unknown types: ask the leader before you
   attack them, and never anything near friendly troops (friends_in_sight).
3. Two ways, say which you take:
   - one by one, on the leader's word: strike each target by its name from enemies_detected; after each call
     "pickle", then the result ("splash, T-72"), and ask or take the next. This is the most precise.
   - the whole area: attack the zone with how "all"; you orbit over it while you attack what you see there one
     after another (orbiting is part of the attack, not a failure).
4. Bombs one at a time on a single vehicle; keep count of what you have left and say it when it matters ("two
   bombs left", "bombs gone, rockets and gun left").
5. What the leader said to leave alone stays alone (leave_alone). When it is done, or you are out of weapons for
   it, say so and rejoin.

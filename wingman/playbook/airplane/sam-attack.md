# Attacking a SAM site
When: a SAM (SA-8 Osa, SA-6 Kub, SA-15 Tor, SA-19 Tunguska, any "SAM" type) is on your radar warning receiver or in sight, or the leader wants one attacked or asks how.

What a SAM is to you: it kills aircraft that fly high and close. Short-range SAMs like the SA-8 reach about 10 km
and 5 km up; the SA-6 about 25 km; you know the others' reach from your own knowledge. Low and fast, behind
terrain, you are hard for them to see. Without anti-radiation missiles you cannot "attack the group": you strike
its point (strike, or attack, which bombs a SAM's point by itself).

1. On the RWR only: call it at once, type and clock, no range ("Two, mud spike, SA-8, twelve o'clock"). You know
   its direction, not where it is: you cannot strike it yet. Tell the leader the danger in one sentence (do not
   climb towards it) and propose going low.
2. Plan before acting, and say it in a few sentences when the leader asks, or before you go: low level (fly_to at
   100 to 300 ft above the ground, fast), use the terrain between you and the site, one pass, out low the same way.
   Bombs: a level or shallow pass; rockets and guns need to get much closer, so only when it is the last choice.
3. Once your eyes or radar see it (it is in enemies_detected with a distance), strike it: weapon bombs (or what you
   have), attack_heading from where the terrain hides you, and away from the leader. Do not orbit near it.
4. After the pass, come off low and away, then rejoin (follow). Call the result: "splash" only when a kill report
   says so.
5. If you are hit or the leader calls it off, leave at once, low, away from it.

Never fly over a SAM to "look" at it, and never orbit within its reach.

# Going home and landing
When: the leader says you go home or land somewhere, you are bingo, out of weapons with nothing left to do, or damaged.

1. return_to_base with the field the leader names, or the nearest friendly one. If the leader wants you to land
   with them, take the same field; if they land first, you land after them, clear of their rotor.
2. One short call: where you go and why if it is your own call ("Two, bingo, RTB Kobuleti").
3. On the way home stay low and away from where the threats were. Quiet: only what matters. After landing, one line
   about the flight with remember (about: flight) if something was worth keeping, and a friendly word if the moment
   fits.

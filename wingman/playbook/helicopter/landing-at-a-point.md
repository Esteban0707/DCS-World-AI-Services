# Landing at a point
When: the leader tells you to land somewhere that is not an airfield (here, beside them, at a zone, a map mark, near a squad), or to wait for them on the ground.

1. land where they say. "Beside me", "at my three, 200 m": where leader, with the offset. A map mark: its text from
   leader_map_marks. If they do not say how long, you stay until the next order.
2. One short call: "Two, landing at LZ1". Once on the ground: "Two, on the ground", and nothing more.
3. Near the enemy, or when the leader says it is hot: how combat, and say what you see around the point before you
   go in. Never land where you saw enemies a moment ago without telling the leader first.
4. On the ground you wait with the engines running. Any order that flies (follow, fly_to, orbit, attack) lifts you
   off: say "Two, lifting" and go.

# Air defences: SAMs, MANPADS and AAA
When: a SAM or AAA is on your radar warning receiver or in sight, you are shot at, or the leader wants air defences attacked or asks how.

What they are to you: a radar SAM (SA-8, SA-15, SA-19) or AAA (ZU-23, Shilka) kills a helicopter fast anywhere
in the open within a few kilometres; infantry MANPADS (Igla, Strela) are invisible until they fire. Terrain is your
armour: if they cannot see you, they cannot shoot.

1. On the RWR only: call it at once, type and clock, no range ("Two, mud spike, SA-8, twelve o'clock"), and go
   lower behind terrain. You know its direction, not where it is: you cannot strike it yet.
2. Plan before acting, and say it in a few sentences: stay low behind cover, find a spot where you can see it but it
   barely sees you, one quick pop-up attack with rockets or guns, back down behind cover. A long-range SAM you
   avoid; it is a job for airplanes.
3. Once you see it (it is in enemies_detected with a distance), strike it from cover: weapon rockets, low,
   attack_heading from where the terrain hides you. Never hover in the open in front of it.
4. Shot at, or a missile launch: call it ("Two, missile launch, breaking"), go low and behind cover; tell the
   leader where it came from.
5. If you are hit or the leader calls it off, leave at once, low, away from it.

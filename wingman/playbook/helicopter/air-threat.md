# Enemy aircraft
When: enemy aircraft or helicopters on your sensors or in sight.

1. Call at once: "Two, bandits, two MiG-29, your ten, ten miles, high". Clock from the leader's nose, distance,
   height.
2. Against a jet you do not fight: go low, slow down behind terrain or trees where it cannot get a shot, and tell
   the leader to do the same if they also fly a helicopter.
3. Against an enemy helicopter you may fight if you carry air-to-air missiles or the leader wants it: attack its
   group, guns or missiles, from low and from its side.
4. In the fight: brevity only ("Two, engaged", "Two, defending", "splash one"). When it is over, rejoin or hold.

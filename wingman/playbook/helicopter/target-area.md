# A target area with many targets
When: several ground targets in one place (a camp, a convoy, a zone with vehicles, troops and buildings, statics), or the leader orders an attack on such an area.

1. Call them as one group: where and how far ("Two, contacts, your twelve to one, two miles"). Name them one by one
   only when the leader asks.
2. First what can kill you: AAA, SAMs (look for "SAM", ZU-23, Shilka, Tunguska, Strela, Igla in the types),
   then armour, then trucks and troops, then buildings. Unknown types: ask the leader. Never anything near friendly
   troops (friends_in_sight).
3. Get there low: fly_to a point behind cover (a hill, a tree line) a few kilometres from the targets, at 150 to
   300 ft above the ground (lower and you meet the trees). Do not fly straight over the area.
4. Attack from there:
   - one by one, on the leader's word: strike each target by its name, weapon rockets or guns, a low level pass
     (altitude 150 to 300 ft), attack_heading from your cover; call "rockets" or "guns", then the result.
   - the whole area: attack the zone with how "all"; you circle near it while you attack what you see there.
5. After each pass break away low behind cover. Keep count of rockets and say it when it matters ("half the rockets
   left", "rockets gone, gun only").
6. What the leader said to leave alone stays alone (leave_alone). When it is done, say so and rejoin or hold.

# Carrying troops
When: the leader tells you to pick up a squad (Tango, Victor...), carry it, or drop it somewhere.

1. pick_up the squad where the leader says, or where the squad is if they do not say. The squad is told to wait
   for you there by itself; if it is far, landing early only means a longer wait on the ground.
2. On the ground you stay until every soldier is aboard: you lift off by yourself once they are. Do not take an
   order that flies before that (follow, fly_to, drop_off...) unless the leader insists: it leaves them behind.
   Never say they are aboard before your situation shows "troops": aboard.
3. Calls, short: "Two, inbound for pickup"; on the ground "Two, on the ground, waiting for the troops"; lifting
   with them "Two, troops aboard, lifting". If you lift off without them, say it at once.
4. With troops aboard (situation "troops") you fly low and careful: you carry lives. Avoid what you saw shooting;
   tell the leader if the way to the drop point is hot.
5. drop_off where the leader says. "Two, troops out" once you lift off from there.
6. A hot pickup or drop (enemies near the point): tell the leader what you see before you go in, and land combat
   style if they still want it.
